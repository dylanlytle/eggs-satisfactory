# Efficiency Group for Guaranteed Satisfaction

> The E.G.G.S. cooperative campaign handbook for 4-8 pioneers. Cooperation is voluntary. Satisfaction is guaranteed.

## Operating model

Use a federation of specialized regional factories, not one enormous central factory. Each player owns an industrial department while the central Nexus provides coordination, shared construction supplies, transit, research, and Project Assembly.

## The Nexus

Build the HUB, MAM, AWESOME Shop/Sink, workshop, Space Elevator, construction mall, Dimensional Depot uploaders, passenger interchange, and objective board at the Nexus.

Ship building supplies, milestone materials, Project Parts, equipment, ammunition, filters, packaged fuel, and small advanced-component buffers to the Nexus. Keep ores, most ingots, screws, silica, petroleum coke, high-volume intermediates, and balanced fluid loops regional.

## Role cards

### The Mayor of Spaghetti - NEXUS OPERATIONS

**Owns:** The Nexus, Space Elevator, shared storage, milestone launches

**Standing orders**

- Maintain the current-objective board and shortage list.
- Keep the construction mall and Dimensional Depot uploaders stocked.
- Reserve six labeled Elevator input lanes.
- Track requested versus delivered Project Parts.
- Organize the group photo before every Elevator launch.

**Campaign goals**

- Early: establish the Nexus and automate Smart Plating.
- Midgame: build the Project Assembly campus.
- Late: coordinate Phases 4 and 5 without trying to make everything personally.

> **Remember:** The Mayor owns coordination, not everybody else's output.

### The Grid Goblin - INFRASTRUCTURE

**Owns:** Foundations, roads, railways, hypertubes, blueprints, build standards

**Standing orders**

- Establish the world-grid datum at the Nexus.
- Connect regional factories with roads and power corridors.
- Create station, intersection, belt-bridge, pipe-rack, and rail-support blueprints.
- Leave expansion space beside every major corridor.
- Provide passenger access as well as freight access.

**Campaign goals**

- Early: grid anchors, roads, and power corridors.
- Phase 2: hypertube backbone and shared blueprints.
- Phase 3 onward: double-track rail ring, standardized stations, then drones.

> **Remember:** A corridor without expansion room is future spaghetti in larval form.

### The Iron Tyrant - STRUCTURAL INDUSTRY

**Owns:** Iron, copper, limestone, steel, and structural components

**Standing orders**

- Supply core building materials, rotors, frames, beams, pipes, stators, and motors.
- Maintain separate construction-stock and production-contract outputs.
- Use expandable production blocks instead of one supposedly permanent factory.
- Coordinate Heavy and Fused Modular Frames with electronics and aluminum.

**Campaign goals**

- Phase 1: all 50 Smart Plating.
- Phase 2: Smart Plating and Versatile Framework.
- Phase 3: motors and Heavy Modular Frames.
- Late: Fused Frames and Pressure Conversion Cubes with the Aluminati.

> **Remember:** Never let a milestone consume the last shared stack of building supplies.

### The Watt Wrangler - POWER AUTHORITY

**Owns:** Generation, grid stability, fuel delivery, and emergency recovery

**Standing orders**

- Rush automated coal power and maintain a black-start kit.
- Separate essential production, transit, and decorative loads where practical.
- Keep roughly 20-25 percent comfortable generating headroom.
- Post generation, maximum consumption, and reserve at the Nexus.
- Approve major new loads after their owners announce expected consumption.

**Campaign goals**

- Early: organized biomass, then coal.
- Midgame: fuel or turbofuel expansion.
- Phase 3: large grid upgrade for aluminum and advanced production.
- Late: nuclear or an intentionally oversized fuel alternative.

> **Remember:** Power capacity is a product. Manufacture it before the factory needs it.

### Baron von Crude - PETROCHEMICALS

**Owns:** Oil, plastic, rubber, packaged fluids, and fuel products

**Standing orders**

- Build reliable plastic and rubber contracts before clever recycling loops.
- Coordinate all generator fuel commitments with the Watt Wrangler.
- Label every pipe with fluid, direction, and expected rate.
- Provide packaged fuel for explorers and jetpack users.
- Never mix fluids in one pipe network.

**Campaign goals**

- Phase 2: scout and reserve the oil site and transport route.
- Phase 3: plastic, rubber, fuel, and Modular Engine inputs.
- Phase 4: support Turbo Motors, Cooling Systems, and rockets.
- Phase 5: supply petrochemical inputs for final parts.

> **Remember:** Fluids remember every bad decision.

### The Circuit Whisperer - ELECTRONICS

**Owns:** Caterium, quartz, electronics, computers, and communications technology

**Standing orders**

- Produce quickwire, silica, crystal, boards, limiters, computers, and supercomputers.
- Maintain a clearly labeled electronics export depot.
- Select useful alternate recipes with the Slug Marshal.
- Consume quickwire locally when possible instead of exporting huge belt volumes.
- Stock advanced electronics for team equipment and construction.

**Campaign goals**

- Phase 2: Automated Wiring.
- Phase 3: computers and Adaptive Control Unit electronics.
- Phase 4: Assembly Director Systems and Radio Control Units.
- Phase 5: oscillators, neural processors, and AI Expansion Servers.

> **Remember:** Export finished intelligence, not a continent of loose quickwire.

### The Aluminati - ADVANCED MATERIALS

**Owns:** Bauxite, aluminum, nitrogen, cooling systems, and advanced frames

**Standing orders**

- Site the refinery where water and transport are practical.
- Balance water byproducts deliberately and document the recovery route.
- Export casings and sheets rather than raw bauxite.
- Coordinate nitrogen logistics with rail or drones.
- Warn the team before recipe changes that affect fluid balance.

**Campaign goals**

- Phase 3: establish sheets and casings.
- Phase 4: Radio Control Units, Cooling Systems, Fused Frames, and Turbo Motors.
- Phase 5: support Biochemical Sculptors and other Tier 9 production.

> **Remember:** If the refinery's water is balanced, write down why before anybody touches it.

### The Slug Marshal - EXPLORATION & RESEARCH

**Owns:** Exploration, MAM research, hard drives, artifacts, and field supplies

**Standing orders**

- Mark resources, crash sites, caves, geysers, uranium, and future factory sites.
- Gather slugs, hard drives, Mercer Spheres, and Somersloops.
- Advance MAM research and maintain the alternate-recipe shortlist.
- Carry remote-building supplies and rescue death crates.
- Establish radar towers and field shelters.

**Campaign goals**

- Early: map, mobility, inventory, caterium, and quartz research.
- Phases 1-2: hard-drive sweep and Depot research support.
- Phases 3-4: radar coverage and late-resource scouting.
- Phase 5: Somersloop allocation and Tier 9 reconnaissance.

> **Remember:** A marked future resource is more useful than a secret one.

## Campaign roadmap

### Opening Shift - Tiers 0-2

**Company objective:** Get everyone equipped and eliminate hand crafting.

**Work orders**

- Mayor chooses and marks the Nexus site.
- Grid Goblin establishes the grid and starter roads.
- Iron Tyrant automates all basic building materials.
- Watt Wrangler scouts coal and water.
- Slug Marshal unlocks field research and explores.

**Delivery contract**

- Phase 1 delivery: 50 Smart Plating

### The Steel Age - Tiers 3-4

**Company objective:** Coal power, steel, blueprints, and durable logistics.

**Work orders**

- 1,000 Smart Plating

**Work orders**

- Baron establishes plastic, rubber, and fuel.
- Grid Goblin begins the rail backbone.

- Iron Tyrant scales Heavy Modular Frames and motors.

**Delivery contract**

- 2,500 Versatile Framework
- 500 Modular Engines
- 100 Adaptive Control Units

### The Aluminum Conspiracy - Tiers 7-8

**Company objective:** Aluminum, nitrogen, drones, and endgame-scale power.

**Work orders**

- Aluminati establishes balanced aluminum production.
- Regional factories connect by rail; drones serve distant low-volume products.
- Watt Wrangler expands power before Particle Accelerators arrive.

**Delivery contract**

- 500 Assembly Director Systems - Circuit Whisperer

### Operation Probably Saved The Day - Tier 9

**Company objective:** Four project teams, four accountable owners, one extremely expensive button.

**Work orders**

- Keep earlier Project Part factories intact; final parts consume earlier parts.
- Create a signed input contract for each final production line.
- Use the Nexus status wall to track target, stock, rate, and estimated completion.

**Delivery contract**

- Pasta La Vista: 1,000 Nuclear Pasta
- Wet Sculpture Society: 1,000 Biochemical Sculptors
- Definitely Not Skynet: 256 AI Expansion Servers

## The E.G.G.S. Operating Accords

1. **One world grid.** Face a cardinal direction and hold Ctrl when placing the first permanent foundation. Extend from that anchor, use whole-meter elevations, and label the datum foundation GRID ANCHOR - DO NOT DELETE.

2. **Standard corridors.** Roads are at least three foundations wide. Reserve four foundations for a rail trunk. Use double track with right-hand running. Put belts and pipes on dedicated racks and give every factory a passenger entrance.

3. **Factory contracts.** Every export interface states ITEM - RATE/MIN - OWNER - DESTINATION - BELT/PIPE MK. Consumers may rely on contracted output; changes require notice.

4. **Overflow is mandatory.** End production with storage plus Sink, a deliberate standby condition, or a documented overflow route. Important lines must not jam because a container filled overnight.

5. **Construction stock is sacred.** Do not empty the last shared stacks of concrete, plates, cable, beams, pipes, aluminum sheets, or motors into a milestone. Expand production first.

6. **Power changes require notice.** Announce expected consumption before connecting a major factory. Test startup surges, variable power, and fluid behavior after connection.

7. **Name everything.** Use consistent map prefixes: [MINE], [FACTORY], [POWER], [TRANSIT], [FUTURE], and [HELP]. Include the department owner in the name.

8. **Elevator launches are ceremonies.** Wait until everybody can be online. Sound the siren, gather at the Elevator, take the company photo, then launch.

9. **Done has a definition.** A finished factory runs under load, has stable power and fluids, labeled interfaces, safe access, an overflow policy, and space or a plan for expansion.

10. **Fun outranks perfect efficiency.** No unsolicited demolition. Museums, mascots, hypertube cannons, questionable statues, and weird architecture are protected cultural assets.

## Session checklist

### Start of session

- Read the Nexus objective board.
- Check power reserve before adding loads.
- Claim one concrete deliverable.
- Tell the owner before modifying a factory.
- Announce major recipe or power changes.

### End of session

- Update output rates and shortage signs.
- Restock shared construction materials.
- Park vehicles and clear blocked tracks.
- Mark unfinished work with [HELP] or [FUTURE].
- Save Elevator celebrations for the full team.
